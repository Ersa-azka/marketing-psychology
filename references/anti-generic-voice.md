# The De-AI System — Making Copy Sound Human

The single biggest reason marketing content underperforms today: it reads as
AI-generated. Readers can smell it, and the smell means "template, low-effort,
don't trust." This file is the full system for stripping the AI signature and
injecting a real human voice. Run it on every deliverable.

## Why generic copy fails

Generic copy is safe, symmetrical, and says nothing a competitor couldn't say.
It optimizes for "sounds like marketing" instead of "sounds like a specific
person who knows this thing." Persuasion lives in specificity and conviction —
exactly what generic copy sands off.

---

## The tells — a hunting checklist

Scan the draft and kill these:

**Structural tells**
- Warm-up openers: "Di era digital ini…", "Di zaman serba cepat…", "In today's
  world…", "Bayangkan sebuah…", "Mari kita…".
- The tricolon reflex — everything bundled in threes ("cepat, mudah, dan
  terjangkau").
- "Bukan hanya… tapi juga…" / "not only… but also…" as filler scaffolding.
- Tidy summary endings that restate the whole message in a bow.
- Every paragraph the same length and rhythm.

**Word-level tells**
- Hollow superlatives with no proof: "berkualitas tinggi", "terbaik",
  "terpercaya", "revolusioner", "solusi tepat", "premium", "tak tertandingi".
- Inspiration filler: "wujudkan impian", "raih potensi", "elevate", "unlock",
  "transform your…".
- Corporate throat-clearing: "Kami dengan bangga mempersembahkan…", "Sebagai
  penyedia terkemuka…".
- Overuse of em-dashes; the "It's not just X, it's Y" construction on repeat.

**Voice tells**
- No opinion, no edge, no enemy — perfectly neutral.
- No detail a real insider would know.
- Reads fine but no real person talks like this.

---

## The fixes — inject humanity

**1. Specificity.** Swap every abstraction for a concrete.
- ❌ "Bahan berkualitas, tahan lama."
- ✅ "Jati Perhutani umur 20 tahun, dikeringkan oven sampai 12%. Gak melengkung
  walau AC-nya kenceng."

**2. A point of view.** Take a side; name a real enemy.
- ❌ "Kami menyediakan furniture berkualitas dengan harga bersaing."
- ✅ "Kebanyakan 'jati' di marketplace itu jati muda dicat gelap. 2 tahun retak.
  Kami gak main di situ."

**3. Rhythm variation.** Break the metronome. Long sentence, then a short jab.
- ✅ "Kami ukur, potong, rakit, finishing, semua di workshop sendiri di
  Jepara — gak ada tangan ketiga. Hasilnya kelihatan."

**4. Insider detail.** One line only someone in the trade would say. This is the
strongest single anti-generic move.
- ✅ "Sambungannya pakai sistem pasak, bukan cuma lem dan sekrup — makanya masih
  kokoh pas cucu kamu yang pakai."

**5. Real voice.** Write how the actual seller/audience talks. Read it aloud in
your head. If it sounds like a brochure, rewrite until it sounds like a person
who cares.

**6. Controlled imperfection.** A fragment. A parenthetical aside. A casual word
where a corporate one would go. Not sloppy — textured.

## Before / after

**Before (generic AI):**
> "Di era modern ini, memiliki furniture berkualitas tinggi adalah suatu
> keharusan. Kami hadir menyediakan produk terbaik dengan bahan pilihan dan
> harga terjangkau untuk mewujudkan hunian impian Anda. Percayakan kebutuhan
> furniture Anda kepada kami!"

**After (human, specific, opinionated):**
> "Beli meja jati sekali, harusnya buat 20 tahun. Masalahnya, banyak yang jual
> jati muda dicat gelap — 2 tahun udah retak, warnanya belang. Punya kami dari
> jati tua, kering oven 12%, sambungan pasak. Mau lihat dulu di workshop sebelum
> mutusin? Boleh banget."

Notice: a named enemy, real specifics (20 tahun, 12%, pasak), a rhythm shift, a
low-risk CTA, zero hollow adjectives.

---

## Final delivery checklist

Before sending anything, confirm:

- [ ] Opens with a hook, not a warm-up.
- [ ] Built on ONE clear persuasion lever.
- [ ] At least one specific, checkable detail (number/name/place/texture).
- [ ] Has a point of view — an opinion or a named enemy.
- [ ] Rhythm varies; not all sentences the same length.
- [ ] Zero hollow superlatives without proof next to them.
- [ ] Sounds like a real person of this trade talking to this audience.
- [ ] One clear, low-risk CTA.
- [ ] Every claim is TRUE (no fake scarcity/proof/authority).

If any box fails, it's not done.
