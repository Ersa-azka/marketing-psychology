# Copywriting Frameworks & Structure

Frameworks are scaffolding, not scripts. Use them to structure persuasion, then
voice them so no one can tell a framework was used. If the reader can feel the
skeleton, you've failed.

## 1. The four core frames

**PAS — Problem, Agitate, Solve.** Best for pain-driven offers.
- Problem: name the exact pain in their words.
- Agitate: twist it — the hidden cost, what it's really costing them, where it
  leads if ignored. (Don't be cruel; be honest about stakes.)
- Solve: your offer as the specific relief.

*Mini-example (jasa perbaikan AC):*
"AC bocor netes ke plafon. Dibiarin seminggu — plafon gips mulai kuning, ganti
plafon 1,5 juta. Padahal servis bocornya 150rb. Kita datang hari ini juga."

**AIDA — Attention, Interest, Desire, Action.** General workhorse for ads/posts.
Grab → make relevant → make wanted → tell them what to do.

**BAB — Before, After, Bridge.** Best for transformation offers.
- Before: their current frustrating reality.
- After: the vivid, specific desired state.
- Bridge: your product is how they cross.

*Mini-example (kelas online):*
"Sekarang: bikin caption 45 menit, tetep sepi. Nanti: 10 menit, komen rame.
Jembatannya: 12 template hook yang udah teruji, kamu tinggal isi."

**FAB — Feature, Advantage, Benefit.** For technical/feature-heavy products.
Never stop at the feature. Feature (what it is) → Advantage (what it does) →
Benefit (what that means for *them*, emotionally). "Kadar air 12% (F) → gak
nyusut/melengkung (A) → meja kamu masih rata 10 tahun lagi, gak malu pas ada
tamu (B)."

## 2. Hooks — the first 2 seconds

Scroll-stopping openers. Rotate types; never open the same way twice in a
campaign:

- **Specific claim**: "Naik dari 3 order jadi 40 order/hari, tanpa iklan."
- **Callout**: "Buat kamu yang jualan di IG tapi DM sepi —"
- **Pattern-break / contrarian**: "Diskon itu bikin brand kamu murah. Serius."
- **Open loop**: "Ada 1 kalimat yang bikin closing-ku naik 2x. Bukan soal
  harga."
- **Story cold-open**: "Jam 11 malam, pembeli itu masih nawar. Terus dia
  bilang…"
- **Number/list promise**: "3 kata yang jangan pernah kamu tulis di caption
  jualan."

Never open with: definitions, "di era…", warm-ups, or self-introduction.

## 3. Headlines

The headline does 80% of the work. Test it against: is it specific, is there a
clear benefit or intrigue, does it target one reader? Reliable structures:

- **How to [result] without [pain/objection]** — "Cara dapat klien luar negeri
  tanpa jago bahasa Inggris."
- **[Number] [things] that [outcome]** — "5 kesalahan harga yang bikin custom
  order lari."
- **The reason [surprising truth]** — "Alasan kenapa produk kamu bagus tapi gak
  laku."
- **[Do desirable thing] in [timeframe]** — real timeframes only.
- **Question that hits the pain** — used sparingly, not as a default.

Cut every word that doesn't earn its place. Specific > clever.

---

## 4. Open loops & retention

Curiosity is a gap between what they know and want to know. Open a loop early
("nanti aku kasih tau bagian yang paling sering disepelein"), pay it off later.
In scripts/reels, tease the payoff before the value so they stay. Never open a
loop you don't close — that's clickbait, and it burns trust.

---

## 5. CTA design

- **One** CTA per piece. Choices kill action.
- Make the next step **small and low-risk**: "tanya-tanya dulu, gratis", "cek
  ketersediaan", "DM 'JATI'". A big ask ("beli sekarang") to a cold reader
  fails; a tiny ask starts the commitment ladder.
- **Reduce risk** at the point of action: guarantee, easy exit, "gak cocok,
  balikin".
- **Tell them exactly what happens next** — uncertainty stops clicks.
- Add a real reason to act now (true scarcity/deadline) only if one exists.

---

## 6. Offer structuring

The offer often matters more than the copy. Strengthen it before you polish
words:

- **Stack value** so price feels small vs. what they get; then reveal price.
- **Anchor** against a higher number first (full price, competitor, cost of the
  problem).
- **Risk reversal** — the stronger the guarantee, the weaker their hesitation.
- **Bonuses** that solve the *next* problem they'll hit.
- **A reason for the price/deal** — an honest "kenapa lagi promo" beats an
  unexplained discount, which reads as either desperation or fake.
- **Decoy tier** to steer toward your target package.

A great offer with average copy beats great copy on a weak offer. Fix the offer
first.
